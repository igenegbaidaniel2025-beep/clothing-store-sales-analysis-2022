2022 Clothing Store Sales Analysis

Overview

A 2022 sales and order performance analysis for a clothing store, created to help understand sales trends, customer characteristics, order performance, and business performance across different channels and product categories.

Business Objective

The goal was to analyze the store's 2022 sales and orders and provide a clear view of what was happening across customers, products, channels, locations, and order status.

Analysis Covered

- 📊 Orders vs. Sales performance
- 👕 Sales comparison between men and women
- 📱 Orders by sales channel
- 🛍️ Sales by product category
- 📅 Monthly sales and order trends
- 🚚 Order status and delivery performance
- 👥 Customer age groups and gender
- 📍 Top-performing states

Dashboard

The analysis was presented through an interactive Excel dashboard designed to make the results easier to understand and use for business decision-making.

Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Pivot Tables
- Data visualization
- Dashboard design

Key Business Questions

- Which months generated the strongest sales?
- Which customer groups contributed most to sales?
- Which sales channels performed best?
- Which product categories performed best?
- What proportion of orders were successfully delivered?
- Which states generated the most sales?

Project Files

- "analysis.xlsx" - Full analysis and dashboard
- Dashboard images - Visual representation of the analysis

Business Relevance

The analysis provides a practical view of sales and order performance that can help a clothing business identify trends, understand customers, evaluate sales channels, and make better-informed decisions.
